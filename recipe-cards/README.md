# Bakits Junior recipe-card templates

These six A4 templates are designed for younger bakers with a polished Bakits look. Open `index.html` in a browser, click any light-grey prompt to replace it, and use **Print recipe cards** when the recipes are complete.

Each card includes:

- large, numbered steps with one action per step;
- a grown-up helper and safety callout;
- separate checklists for kit ingredients and items from home;
- restrained color-coding and completion checkboxes;
- editable recipe directions and tips;
- A4 print styling with one recipe per page.

For permanent content changes, edit `recipes.js`. Replace the shared placeholder content once the tested ingredient quantities, oven settings, allergens, and directions are available.
